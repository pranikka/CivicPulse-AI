Responsible AI & Transparency

How we ensure AI recommendations serve citizen interests

🤖 AI-Generated, Not AI-Decided

Every AI recommendation is flagged for human review. AI does not make final decisions. Government officers and planners review, verify, and make final choices.

🔍 Explainability

For every recommendation, we show the reasoning: citizen demand metrics, infrastructure gap scores, population impact, and alignment with existing plans.

👥 Human Oversight

Every request undergoes manual review by trained government officers who verify AI analysis and make informed decisions.

📊 Data Privacy

Citizen identities are protected. Analysis is performed at the aggregate and geographic level, not individual. No personal data is exposed.

Inclusive & Accessible

Built for all citizens, regardless of language or digital literacy

🌐 Multilingual Support

Requests accepted in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam. AI automatically detects and processes language.

🎙️ Voice Input Option

Submit requests via voice when text input is inconvenient (demo feature).

♿ Accessible Design

WCAG-compliant design with high contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader support.
